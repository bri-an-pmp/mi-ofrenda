Día de los Muertos · 1 y 2 de noviembre

Build an altar. Aprende español.

On the Day of the Dead, families in Mexico build an ofrenda (altar) to welcome the spirits of the people they love back home for one night.

A traditional altar has three steps: heaven at the top, earth in the middle, and the underworld at the bottom. A marigold arch marks the doorway between the living and the dead.

Choose someone to remember, then place offerings on their altar. Each one teaches you its Spanish word, how to say it, and how to use it in a sentence.
